## 🌟 Overview — The Vision Behind the Sanctuary

Welcome to **FormAI Sanctuary**, a next-generation computational movement studio that transforms how human beings relate to their own bodies. While its spiritual ancestor, *FormAI*, introduced the world to AI-guided personal training, **FormAI Sanctuary** goes further — it does not merely *watch* your form; it *composes* it. Think of it as a master choreographer, a biomechanics professor, and a patient mentor fused into a single, ambient intelligence that lives quietly in your browser, your phone, and your wearable ecosystem.

This repository houses the complete architecture of the Sanctuary: a real-time pose estimation engine, a procedural feedback composer, an adaptive progression planner, and a beautifully responsive multisensory interface. It is designed for athletes, physiotherapy patients, dancers, rehabilitation specialists, weekend warriors, and anyone who has ever wondered, *"Am I actually doing this exercise correctly?"*

The answer, from now on, lives here.

## 🌱 Why FormAI Sanctuary Exists

Most fitness software counts repetitions. A rare few estimate calories. Almost none actually understand **movement quality** — the subtle interplay between joint angles, tempo, symmetry, and muscular intent that separates a *productive* session from an *injury waiting to happen*.

FormAI Sanctuary was born from a frustration shared by coaches, clinicians, and athletes alike: cameras became smart, but coaching did not. We asked ourselves a heretical question:

> *What if your device could describe — in plain human language — exactly how your left knee drifted three degrees too far inward on rep fourteen of your squat, and why that matters for your patellar tendon?*

The Sanctuary answers that question in milliseconds, continuously, and without judgment. It is not a judge. It is a mirror that speaks.

## 🎛️ Core Feature Constellation

Every feature below is engineered to dissolve friction between the human body and the digital coach. Read them as invitations, not specifications.

- **Real-Time Pose Cartography** — A dense skeletal mesh is reconstructed from your camera feed at up to 60 frames per second, giving instant awareness of joint trajectories and limb relationships.
- **Tempo Choreography Engine** — Detects eccentric, concentric, and isometric phases, then narrates pacing back to you in elegant, human language.
- **Symmetry Sentinel** — Highlights left/right imbalances as they emerge, not after the fact, so compensations never become habits.
- **Adaptive Progression Composer** — Rebuilds the next session dynamically based on today's performance, fatigue signals, and your declared goals.
- **Responsive Interface, Everywhere** — A fluid layout that restructures itself gracefully across ultrawide monitors, laptops, tablets, foldables, and wrist devices.
- **Multilingual Conscience** — Coaching cues and interface copy are localized for global audiences, with tone-appropriate phrasing per culture.
- **Ambient Session Memory** — Summaries are stored locally so you can watch progress bloom over weeks rather than increments.
- **Voice & Text Duality** — Choose a warm spoken coach, a silent textual whisper, or both.
- **Safety Envelope Detection** — Recognizes dangerous joint configurations and interrupts with clear, calm guidance.
- **Offline Humility Mode** — Delivers core feedback without any network dependency, because your training should not hinge on a signal bar.
- **Wearable Whispers** — Pulse rate, HRV, and motion sensors from compatible wearables enrich the coaching context.
- **Coach Console** — Professionals can observe multiple clients in a live roster with annotated overlays.
- **Session Storytelling** — Turns raw metrics into a readable narrative — a paragraph, not a spreadsheet.

## 🧠 The Movement Intelligence Layer

At the heart of the Sanctuary lives what we call the **Movement Intelligence Layer (MIL)**. It is a layered system of perception, interpretation, and expression:

1. **Perception Tier** — Captures frames, extracts landmarks, smooths noise, and maintains a temporal identity of each joint.
2. **Interpretation Tier** — Compares the observed motion against a library of canonical templates, but with tolerance — because no two bodies are identical.
3. **Expression Tier** — Converts classification into a coaching cue. This is where the Sanctuary's voice lives: concise, kind, and specific.

The MIL is deliberately modular. Researchers can swap classifiers, therapists can author custom templates, and developers can extend expression vocabularies without touching perception code.

## 🎨 User Experience Philosophy

FormAI Sanctuary refuses the aesthetic of a control panel. Instead, the interface borrows from ateliers, studios, and rehearsal halls:

- **Quiet Defaults** — The system speaks only when something meaningful changes.
- **Calm Color Semantics** — Warm hues for encouragement, cool hues for neutral observation, and a gentle amber for attention.
- **Typography With Breath** — Generous line height, abundant whitespace, and clear hierarchy so the eye never fights the interface.
- **Motion That Supports Meaning** — Transitions illuminate bone-joint overlays rather than distracting from them.
- **No Shame Architecture** — The feedback tone is deliberately non-punitive. Nothing in the Sanctuary says "wrong." It says "here is another path."

## 🌍 Multilingual & Accessibility Charter

The Sanctuary treats language as a first-class architectural concern, not a translation afterthought.

- **Localized Coaching Cues** — Idiomatic phrasings are authored per language, not machine-translated blindly.
- **Right-to-Left Harmony** — Interfaces mirror cleanly for Arabic, Hebrew, and Farsi.
- **Dyslexia-Friendly Reading Mode** — Optional spacing and font treatment for comfortable reading.
- **High-Contrast Practice Mode** — Designed for gyms with harsh lighting or users with visual sensitivities.
- **Screen Reader Narration** — All dynamic feedback is announced with ARIA-compliant labels.
- **Keyboard-Only Rituals** — Every action is reachable without a mouse.
- **Subtitled Audio Cues** — For shared spaces and silent environments.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

The Sanctuary is assembled from carefully chosen building blocks, each picked for durability rather than novelty:

- **Runtime Layer** — Modern JavaScript/TypeScript for the client, with a lightweight Node-compatible service layer.
- **Vision Pipeline** — WebGL-accelerated landmark inference with adaptive frame skipping on constrained hardware.
- **State Architecture** — Deterministic session state modeled as an event-sourced timeline for full replayability.
- **Data Layer** — Local-first storage using IndexedDB, with optional encrypted sync to a self-hosted endpoint.
- **Styling System** — Token-based design system enabling themes, contrast modes, and layout inversions.
- **Build & Tooling** — Reproducible builds with bundler-agnostic configuration and lightweight test harness.
- **Telemetry Discipline** — Opt-in only; never collects video, and never streams frames off the device.

## ❓ Frequently Explored Questions

- **Does the Sanctuary store my video?** No. Frames are processed on your device and discarded after inference.
- **Does it work without internet access?** Yes; core feedback runs fully offline.
- **Can clinicians define their own movement templates?** Absolutely; the template format is documented and open.
- **Is it usable on phones?** Yes; the responsive UI adapts gracefully from wide monitors to handheld devices.
- **Is there a cost?** The project is openly licensed for personal, research, and clinical use.
- **Can I contribute a translation?** Yes; translations live in structured resource bundles in /ui.
- **Which wearables are supported?** A growing roster; the integration layer is adapter-based.
